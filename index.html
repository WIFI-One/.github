Why WIFI One

Private by construction.

Built local-first so your data never has to leave the room.

Open a browser and connect. Nothing to sign up for, no passwords, and temporary display names only.

Works offline

Once loaded, an internet outage won't stop you. If Wi-Fi is up, WIFI One keeps working.
